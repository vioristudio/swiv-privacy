Swiv Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Swiv is a personal diary and daily record app provided by Viori Studio. This page explains how Swiv handles information.

1. Who we are

Swiv is provided by Viori Studio. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at privacy@swivhome.com.

2. What Swiv does

Swiv helps users keep personal diary entries, moods, memories, notes, daily images, schedules, todos, logs, tags, diary pages, stickers, comments, and other personal daily records.

3. Diary content and user records

Swiv may allow users to create and store personal diary content, including but not limited to:

  • diary text
  • moods
  • weather selections
  • tags
  • memos
  • todos
  • events, schedules, and timetable items
  • logs
  • comments
  • diary pages
  • Today’s Image / Daily Image added by the user
  • stickers and page decorations
  • app settings and customization choices

This diary content is stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to back up, share, or transfer it using device features or third-party apps.

Viori Studio does not operate a server-side diary account system for Swiv. Viori Studio does not receive, store, read, sell, or share users’ diary entries, images, tags, moods, notes, schedules, or other personal diary content through its own servers.

4. Local storage

Swiv may store app data locally on the user’s device to provide normal app functionality. This may include diary records, daily images, app settings, theme settings, font settings, backup-related settings, and similar app-use data.

Locally stored app data can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app from the device. If the user has created backup files, those files may remain wherever the user saved or shared them.

Because Viori Studio does not operate a server-side diary account system for Swiv, there is no server-stored diary account data for users to request deletion of.

5. Images and files

Swiv may allow users to add a Today’s Image / Daily Image from their device as a representative image for a diary day.

These images are stored locally as part of the app’s diary data. Viori Studio does not receive these images through its own servers.

If the user backs up, shares, or transfers diary content that includes images, the selected destination app, cloud service, file manager, email app, messaging app, or operating system feature may process those files according to its own policies.

6. Backup and restore

Swiv may provide backup and restore features depending on the app version.

Backup files are created for the user’s own storage or transfer. These files may contain diary text, daily images, stickers, settings, and other diary-related content included by the backup feature.

Viori Studio does not automatically receive backup files. If the user saves or shares a backup file through another app or service, that third-party app or service is responsible for how it handles the file.

Users should store backup files carefully because they may contain personal diary content.

7. Notifications

Swiv may provide local reminders or notifications depending on the app version and user settings.

Local notifications are used to provide app functionality on the user’s device. Viori Studio does not use its own server to read, store, or send users’ diary notification content.

Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings.

8. Shop and in-app purchases

In the current closed test version, Swiv does not offer active in-app purchases.

The Shop area may be shown as a Coming Soon screen, but Swiv does not display prices, purchase buttons, restore purchase buttons, or active payment flows in this test version.

9. Advertising and Advertising ID

Swiv does not display ads.

Swiv does not use the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Swiv does not collect, access, or use the Android Advertising ID.

Swiv does not use advertising networks or personalized advertising.

10. Accounts and server-side user data

Swiv does not require users to create an account.

Swiv does not provide login, account registration, or account-based cloud diary storage.

Because Viori Studio does not operate user accounts or store user diary data on its own server, there is no server-side account data for us to delete upon request.

Users can delete locally stored app data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app from their device.

11. Analytics and diagnostics

Swiv does not use analytics SDKs to track user behavior.

Swiv does not use third-party crash reporting SDKs that transmit diagnostic data to Viori Studio or third-party analytics services.

12. Third-party services

Swiv may use the following third-party services depending on the app version and platform:

  • Google Play, for app distribution and platform services.
  • Android system services, for device permissions, local notifications, file sharing, backup destinations, and related platform functionality.

Swiv does not currently use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party crash reporting SDKs, or active payment processing flows.

If additional third-party services such as analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, advertising, payment processing, or server-based features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

13. Data sharing

Viori Studio does not sell users’ diary content, images, tags, moods, notes, schedules, or personal records.

Viori Studio does not share user-entered diary content with third parties through its own servers.

Certain information may be processed by platform providers such as Google when users install the app, manage app permissions, receive platform-level services, or use Google Play features. Such processing is governed by the relevant third party’s own policies.

If users choose to back up, send, upload, or share their diary files through another app or service, that action is controlled by the user and the selected destination service.

14. Data retention and deletion

Swiv diary data remains on the user’s device unless the user deletes it, clears app data, restores or overwrites data, removes backup files, or uninstalls the app.

Because Viori Studio does not operate a server-side diary account or diary storage system for Swiv, Viori Studio does not retain users’ diary content on its own servers.

Users are responsible for managing their own local backups.

15. Children’s privacy

Swiv is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through Viori Studio servers.

Because Swiv does not operate user accounts or collect user diary data on Viori Studio servers, Viori Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

16. Security

Swiv is designed as a local-first diary app. Keeping diary data on the user’s device limits Viori Studio’s own access to personal diary content.

However, no method of electronic storage, device storage, backup, or transfer is completely secure. Users should protect their device and backup files carefully, especially because diary content may be personal.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date on this page.

18. Contact

Viori Studio
Privacy contact: privacy@swivhome.com
