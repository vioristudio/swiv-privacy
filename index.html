Swiv Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Swiv is a personal diary and daily record app provided by Viori Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how Swiv handles information in the current app version.

Swiv is designed as a local, private diary app. Your diary records are stored on your device. Viori Studio does not operate a server for collecting, storing, reading, or sharing your diary content.

1. Who we are

Swiv is provided by Viori Studio. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at privacy@swivhome.com.

2. What Swiv does

Swiv helps users keep personal diary entries, moods, memories, notes, todos, events, logs, tags, diary pages, stickers, photos, widgets, backup files, and other personal daily records.

Swiv does not require a login or account in the current version. Swiv does not provide cloud diary storage in the current version.

3. Information Swiv stores locally

Swiv may store app data locally on the user’s device to provide diary and daily record features. This may include:

  • diary records
  • mood records
  • weather records entered or selected by the user
  • tags
  • memo, todo, event, and log records
  • diary pages
  • photos or images added by the user
  • stickers and page decorations
  • stamp information
  • app settings
  • theme, font, and display settings
  • widget-related settings
  • local backup and restore-related data

This data is stored on the user’s device and is used to provide the app’s diary, viewing, customization, widget, backup, and restore features.

4. Diary content and user records

Swiv may allow users to create and save personal diary content, including text, mood, weather, tags, photos, page decorations, and other daily records.

Diary content is stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to back up, share, move, or transfer it using Android system features or third-party apps.

Viori Studio does not operate a server-side diary account system for Swiv. Viori Studio does not receive, upload, store, read, analyze, sell, or share users’ diary entries, images, tags, moods, notes, schedules, or other personal diary content through its own servers.

5. Photos and images

Swiv may allow users to add photos or images to diary pages or daily records.

Photos and images added by the user are stored locally on the device as part of the app data. Viori Studio does not receive these images through its own servers.

If the user shares, backs up, restores, moves, or transfers files using Android system features, the selected destination app, storage provider, file manager, email app, messaging app, cloud service, or operating system feature may process those files according to its own privacy policy.

6. Post screen

Swiv may include a Post screen as an in-app area for app messages, notices, or locally stored app-related content.

The Post screen in the current version is not an email service, social messaging service, or server-based mailbox.

Post-related app content is handled locally within the app. Viori Studio does not use the Post screen to collect, read, transmit, or share users’ diary records.

7. Backup and restore

Swiv may provide local backup and restore features.

Backup files may include diary records, settings, photos, images, stickers, and other app data needed to restore the user’s records.

Backup files are created and handled on the user’s device. Viori Studio does not automatically receive backup files.

If the user chooses to save, move, share, or restore a backup file using Android system features or another app, the selected destination or service is responsible for how it handles that file.

Users should store backup files carefully because they may contain personal diary content.

8. Widgets and notifications

Swiv may provide home screen widget features or local notification features depending on the app version and user settings.

Widget data is generated from local app data on the user’s device.

Local notifications, if enabled, are generated on the user’s device. Swiv does not send diary records to Viori Studio servers for notification processing.

Users can manage widget and notification behavior through app settings and device settings.

9. Advertising and Advertising ID

Swiv does not display ads in the current version.

Swiv does not use the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Swiv does not collect, access, or use the Android Advertising ID.

Swiv does not use advertising networks or personalized advertising.

10. Purchases and shop

The current version of Swiv does not offer in-app purchases, paid items, subscriptions, or an active shop.

If paid digital items, a shop, or in-app purchases are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features become available.

11. Accounts and server-side user data

Swiv does not require users to create an account in the current version.

Swiv does not provide login, account registration, or account-based cloud diary storage.

Because Viori Studio does not operate user accounts or store user diary data on its own server, there is no server-side diary account data for Viori Studio to delete upon request.

Users can delete locally stored app data by deleting records in the app, clearing the app’s data, deleting backup files, or uninstalling the app from their device.

12. Analytics and diagnostics

Swiv does not currently use third-party analytics SDKs.

Swiv does not currently use third-party crash reporting SDKs.

Viori Studio does not collect usage analytics about users’ diary writing, mood records, tags, photos, or personal records.

13. Third-party services

Swiv may use the following third-party services depending on the app version and platform:

  • Google Play, for app distribution and platform services.
  • Android system services, for device permissions, local notifications, file sharing, backup and restore destinations, widgets, and related platform functionality.

Swiv does not currently use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party crash reporting SDKs, or Google Play Billing.

If additional third-party services such as analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, advertising, payment processing, or server-based features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

14. Data sharing

Viori Studio does not sell users’ personal data.

Viori Studio does not share users’ diary content, photos, mood records, tags, notes, schedules, or personal records with advertisers or data brokers.

Viori Studio does not share user-entered diary content with third parties through its own servers.

Certain information may be processed by platform providers such as Google when users install the app, manage app permissions, receive platform-level services, or use Google Play features. Such processing is governed by the relevant third party’s own policies.

If users choose to back up, send, upload, move, or share their diary files through another app or service, that action is controlled by the user and the selected destination service.

15. Data retention and deletion

Swiv diary data remains on the user’s device unless the user deletes it, clears app data, restores or overwrites data, removes backup files, or uninstalls the app.

Because Viori Studio does not operate a server-side diary account or diary storage system for Swiv, Viori Studio does not retain users’ diary content on its own servers.

Users are responsible for managing their own local records and local backups.

16. Device permissions

Swiv may request device permissions only when needed for app features.

Depending on the device, Android version, and app version, this may include permissions related to:

  • selecting or adding photos and images
  • saving or opening backup files
  • using local notifications
  • displaying home screen widgets

Permission availability and behavior may vary depending on Android system settings and device manufacturer behavior.

Users can manage app permissions in their device settings.

17. Children’s privacy

Swiv is not designed for children under the age required by applicable law to use digital services without parental consent.

Swiv does not knowingly collect personal data from children through Viori Studio servers.

18. Security

Swiv is designed as a local-first diary app. Keeping diary data on the user’s device limits Viori Studio’s own access to personal diary content.

However, no method of electronic storage, device storage, backup, or transfer is completely secure. Users should protect their device and backup files carefully, especially because diary content may be personal.

19. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Viori Studio may update this Privacy Policy when Swiv changes, when new features are added, or when legal or platform requirements change.

If changes are made, the effective date on this page will be updated.
